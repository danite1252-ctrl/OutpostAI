One home server, everything you'd use AI for.

Outpost picks models that suit your graphics card and keeps them up to date, so you can get on with using it.

  • Chat with personas

    Each persona has its own instructions, memory and documents. Start from ready-made ones: an assistant, a coder, a researcher and a few just for fun.

  • Pictures, video and 3D

    Make and edit pictures, bring them to life as video, or turn them into 3D models ready for your printer.

  • Talk to it

    Speak instead of typing, hear replies read aloud, or have a hands-free conversation you can interrupt.

  • Coding help that builds

    Give a chat a project folder: it reads the code, makes changes and builds until it works, asking you before it runs anything.

  • On your phone

    The Android app pairs with a QR code. Chat, create and get a notification when a long job is ready.

  • The whole household

    Everyone gets their own account and PIN, and their chats are their own. Children's accounts only reach what you allow.

  • Borrow a bigger graphics card

    A gaming PC can lend its graphics card for big models and long jobs, then go back to gaming when you want it.

  • Tools you choose

    Add tools like web search or your smart home, so a chat can look things up or switch on a lamp when you ask.

Up and running in three steps.

No accounts to make with anyone else, and no subscription.

  1. Install Outpost

    On a Windows PC, or on a Linux server that stays on. It sets up everything it needs and downloads models that fit your hardware.

  2. Pair your phones

    Scan a QR code with the Android app. Your phone then talks only to your Outpost, encrypted.

  3. Invite your household

    Send each person an invite. They choose their own PIN and get their own chats, pictures and personas.
