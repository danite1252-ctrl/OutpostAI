# What's next

Outpost 1.0 is everything planned: chat with personas, memory and documents; Create
(pictures, edits, characters, video, animation, 3D models and picture tools); 3D printing
(.3mf, up to 4 colours, flat bottom or back); Train (on the server or a lent PC); voice; the
phone app (pairing by QR, share to Outpost, saving, a 3D viewer, notifications); the Linux
server with helper PCs and automatic updates; gallery search; and other apps (API, MCP,
webhooks). Since 1.0: renaming chats, naming gallery items (used when they're saved),
phone notifications as soon as a job finishes, and warnings for personas whose model isn't
downloaded.

## Ideas after 1.0

- Training lower-memory settings for 12 GB cards.
- Multi-colour prints with more than 4 filaments, or painted regions kept sharper.
- Notifications on the phone straight away for jobs started elsewhere while the phone app
  hasn't been opened (today those wait for the 15-minute check; jobs seen by the app are
  watched and arrive at once).
