# What's next

Done most recently: the Linux server (phase 6), helper PCs for Create, tidier Settings, the
one-command release (`scripts/release/ship.ps1`), picture tools (upscale and background removal,
from the gallery's picture viewer), the Install ai-toolkit button in Train, and chat with your
documents (PDF, Word, text: attached to a chat or a persona, with sources under each reply),
share to Outpost from any Android app, pairing QR codes that open a download page on a
phone without the app, gallery search by what's in the pictures, automatic server updates, and
a choice of listening model for voice (up to Whisper large-v3-turbo), and 3D models from a
picture (Hunyuan3D 2.1 for a quick shape, TRELLIS.2 for a textured model on a 16 GB card),
shown and turned around in the desktop gallery, saved as .glb for games and 3D apps or (by
default) as a print-ready .3mf for Bambu Studio and other slicers, and saving any gallery item
to the phone (Pictures, Movies or Download, then the share sheet).

## Next, in order

1. **Training on a helper PC** (built, being tested): a lent PC with a 16 GB card and
   ai-toolkit takes trainings the server's card can't, sending each checkpoint and test picture
   back as it's saved. Pausing lending stops a training and keeps the checkpoints so far.
2. **Notifications** when a long job (3D, video, training) finishes or fails.
3. **Multi-colour 3D prints**: TRELLIS.2's colours reduced to at most 4 flat colours, saved
   for Bambu Studio's AMS.
4. **3D viewer on the phone**: turn a model around with a finger before printing it.
5. **Flatten a 3D model's back or base** (an option, off by default) for wall pieces and
   miniatures.
